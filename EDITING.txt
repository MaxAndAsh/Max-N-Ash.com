MAX'N'ASH WEBSITE UPDATE

Included: new chibi logo at the top, two rotating background photos (changes every 30 seconds), music players, and Instagram contact flow.

CONTACT: A static website cannot send a visitor's typed message directly into Instagram DMs. The form copies/prepares the message and opens your Instagram profile; the visitor must paste it into a DM and press Send.

MUSIC DOWNLOADS: The existing music entries are YouTube embeds. YouTube embed URLs do not provide a downloadable MP3 file. To offer direct downloads, place your own MP3 files in assets/music/ and add download links for them. No MP3 files were included in the source website package, so the existing music remains playable but direct MP3 downloads need the audio files.

PUBLISH: Upload the contents of the maxash2 folder to the root of your GitHub Pages repository, replacing the old index.html, style.css, script.js and assets folder.
